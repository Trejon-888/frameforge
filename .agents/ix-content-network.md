## What This Is

The IX Content Network is the phase where kino stops being Enrique's tool and becomes IX's production infrastructure. Instead of one account posting kino content, 10+ IX member accounts post kino-quality content independently — each driving their own audience toward INFINITX.

**The math:**
- Week 1: 1 account (Enrique) posting 5x/week = 5 posts/week
- Week 8: 10 members each posting 3x/week = 30 posts/week
- Week 16: 25 members = 75 posts/week
- All of it ends with `kino · ix`

That's a content network, not a social media account. The market sees IX-quality content everywhere. No one knows it's the same system. They just know IX content is *different*.

## The Coordinated But Not Synchronized Rule

Member content should NOT all post at the same time on the same day.
It should look organic — different people discovering the same amazing quality.
Scooby staggers posts across the week so the market sees a *wave*, not a *blast*.

**Stagger pattern (10-member example):**
- Monday: Members 1, 2 post on LinkedIn
- Tuesday: Members 3, 4 post on X + Reels
- Wednesday: Enrique posts flagship content
- Thursday: Members 5, 6 post
- Friday: Members 7–10 post
- Weekend: Reels/TikTok reposts from the week
